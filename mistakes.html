MONEY MISTAKES

Small Mistakes
Big Impact

Money management is not about being perfect. It is about noticing the everyday choices that can quietly affect your budget.

Explore Common Mistakes

Your money habits are built one decision at a time

Students often manage allowances, part-time income, transport, food, study expenses, subscriptions and everyday purchases. Without a simple plan, small spending decisions can become habits. The first step is simply noticing where your money is going and understanding the choices behind it.

Common Money Mistakes
Students Can Avoid

Open each section to see a realistic situation and a simple action you can take instead.

01
SPENDING HABIT

Impulse Buying

+
THE SITUATION

“It was not planned, but it looked worth it.”

You see something you like and buy it immediately without checking whether the purchase fits your budget.

A BETTER MOVE

Pause before you purchase.

Ask yourself whether you need it, whether you already have something similar, and whether you planned to spend this money.

PAUSE → THINK → DECIDE
02
DAILY SPENDING

Ignoring Small Expenses

+
THE SITUATION

“It is only a small purchase.”

A drink, snack, delivery fee or another small purchase may seem harmless on its own. Repeated spending, however, can take a noticeable part of your monthly money.

A BETTER MOVE

Track the little things.

Write down your small purchases for one week. Seeing the total makes spending patterns much easier to understand.

TRACK → ADD → NOTICE
03
PLANNING HABIT

Late Payments

+
THE SITUATION

“I completely forgot about it.”

An important payment date arrives while you are focused on other expenses. Because it was not included in your plan, you have to rearrange your money at the last moment.

A BETTER MOVE

Keep important dates visible.

Make a simple list or reminder for expected payments so they are considered before spending money elsewhere.

REMEMBER → PLAN → PAY
04
RECURRING SPENDING

Unused Subscriptions

+
THE SITUATION

“I forgot I was still paying for it.”

A subscription continues charging regularly even though you rarely use the service anymore. Repeated charges can quietly increase your monthly spending.

A BETTER MOVE

Review recurring expenses.

Check your subscriptions from time to time. Keep the services that are useful and remove those you no longer need.

CHECK → USE → KEEP
05
BUDGETING HABIT

Spending Without a Plan

+
THE SITUATION

“I will figure it out later.”

Money gets spent throughout the month without deciding how much should go toward requirements, wants and savings.

A BETTER MOVE

Give your money a purpose.

Start with a simple monthly plan. Separate important expenses from optional spending and decide what amount you want to keep for savings.

PLAN → SPEND → SAVE
BEFORE YOU SPEND

Ask yourself
3 Simple Questions.

A quick pause can make a spending decision easier to understand before you commit your money.

01
Do I need this?

Separate important requirements from optional purchases.

02
Did I plan for it?

Check whether the purchase fits your current budget.

03
Will I still value it later?

Give yourself time before making non-essential purchases.
