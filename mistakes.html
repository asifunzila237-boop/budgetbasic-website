KNOW THE RISKS

Common Money Mistakes

Understanding common problems can help you notice unhealthy spending patterns and improve your habits.

01
🛍️

Impulse Buying

Buying something immediately without considering whether you actually need it or whether it fits your budget.

Better Habit Pause before purchasing and ask yourself whether the item is necessary.
02
📋

Not Tracking Expenses

Spending money without keeping track can make it difficult to understand where your income is going.

Better Habit Record your regular expenses and review them at the end of each week.
03
💳

Unplanned Debt

Taking on payments without considering how they fit into your existing monthly budget.

Better Habit Understand the cost and payment commitment before taking on new debt.
04
🐷

Ignoring Savings

Spending all available income without setting aside money for future needs or goals.

Better Habit Include savings as part of your regular financial plan.
05
📱

Small Expenses

Small repeated purchases may seem unimportant, but they can add up over time.

Better Habit Review repeated small purchases and decide which ones you can reduce.
06
📅

No Financial Plan

Making money decisions without a simple plan can make it harder to reach financial goals.

Better Habit Set simple goals and review your budget regularly.
BEFORE YOU SPEND

Ask Yourself These 5 Questions

A short pause before spending can help you make a more thoughtful decision.

01

Do I actually need this?

02

Does it fit my current budget?

03

Do I already own something similar?

04

Could I use this money for an important goal?

05

Would waiting before buying change my decision?
