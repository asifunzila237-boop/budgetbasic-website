Frequently Asked Questions

What is BudgetBasics?

BudgetBasics is a student-friendly financial literacy platform that helps you learn budgeting, saving, spending, and everyday money-management skills.

Is BudgetBasics free to use?

Yes. BudgetBasics is designed to provide students with free access to its financial learning content and helpful money-management tools.

What can I learn on BudgetBasics?

You can learn about budgeting basics, needs and wants, savings goals, common money mistakes, and practical ways to manage your everyday spending.

Does BudgetBasics have money calculators?

Yes. BudgetBasics includes simple calculators that can help you understand your budget, savings goals, and everyday spending.

Can the AI Assistant help me?

The AI Assistant can help explain financial concepts, answer money-related questions, and provide simple guidance based on the information you share.

Who is BudgetBasics made for?

BudgetBasics is primarily designed for students and beginners who want to understand real-world money skills in a simple and practical way.
