SMART SPENDING

Needs vs. Wants

Before spending money, ask yourself whether the purchase is necessary or simply something you want.

🏠

Needs

Things that are important for everyday life and basic responsibilities.

  • ✓ Food
  • ✓ Basic clothing
  • ✓ Housing
  • ✓ Essential transport
VS
🛍️

Wants

Things that may make life enjoyable but are not always essential.

  • ✓ Entertainment
  • ✓ Expensive gadgets
  • ✓ Extra shopping
  • ✓ Luxury items
